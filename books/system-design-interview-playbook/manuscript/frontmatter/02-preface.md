# Preface

System design interviews reward a **repeatable method** more than a memorized zoo of products. This playbook is that method: how to bound the problem, make numbers honest, draw a design you can defend, and go deep where the interviewer actually cares.

I wrote it as an enterprise architect who has sat on both sides of the table—designing platforms, and listening to candidates think out loud. The chapters are built for Git-backed writing: Markdown you can diff, Draw.io diagrams you can revise, Mermaid sequences you can keep next to the prose.

Version 1.0 is the first complete edition of this method plus a small set of fully worked interview problems. Later editions can add more problems without changing the playbook in Part I.

Abdul Hussain  
Enterprise Architect
