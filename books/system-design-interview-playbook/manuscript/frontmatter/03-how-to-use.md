# How to use this book

Read **Part I** once, slowly. The playbook is the product; the later problems are practice.

Use **Part II** as a reference the night before an interview. Each building block is written as: what it is, when you reach for it, which tradeoff sentence to say out loud.

Work **Part III** with a timer. Forty-five minutes, whiteboard or Draw.io, no looking ahead at the sample design until you have your own.

**Part IV** is about the conversation: how to narrate tradeoffs, how to fail a component on purpose, how to close.

Diagrams in this manuscript are first-class. Mermaid is for time-ordered behavior. Draw.io is for the architecture you would actually put on a board.
