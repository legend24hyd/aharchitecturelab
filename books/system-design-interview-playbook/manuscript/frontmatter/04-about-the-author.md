# About the author

**Abdul Hussain** is an Enterprise Architect. His work sits at the intersection of distributed systems, platform design, and the interviews that try to simulate that work in forty-five minutes.

This book is part of AH Architecture Lab, a Git-hosted writing practice for technical books that must survive revision: source in Markdown, diagrams in Draw.io and Mermaid, ebooks compiled for Kindle and other stores.
