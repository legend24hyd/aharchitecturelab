# How system design interviews work

*Outline for Version 1.0 — expand during writing.*

## What the interviewer is scoring

- Problem framing and requirements hygiene
- Ability to estimate without theater
- A coherent high-level design, not a product catalog
- One or two deep dives that show real experience
- Communication: you lead, they steer

## Formats you will meet

- 45-minute single problem
- 60-minute design plus “now make it multi-region”
- Take-home plus live critique (less common)

## What this book will not do

It will not dump vendor feature lists. It will not pretend there is one correct box diagram. It will teach you to **leave a design that can be changed when a constraint changes**.
