# Requirements and scope

*Outline for Version 1.0 — expand during writing.*

## Functional requirements

Write them as user-visible behaviors, not as a shopping list of technologies.

## Non-functional requirements that change designs

Latency, availability, consistency, durability, cost, compliance, team skill. Pick the two that would **change the diagram** if they moved.

## Scope control

- MVP vs later
- Explicit non-goals
- “If we have time” parking lot so you do not disappear into SSO

## Clarifying questions worth asking

Traffic shape, read/write ratio, object size, geo, offline clients, existing systems you must reuse.
